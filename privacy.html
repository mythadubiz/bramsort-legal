Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Bramsort: Potion Puzzle ("the app", "we") handles your information. By using the app you agree to this policy.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress — level record, unlocked elixirs (Grimoire), unlocked Journal pages, unlocked companions, coin balance and settings — is stored locally on your device (and in your personal iCloud if enabled). We do not run our own servers and we do not collect this data.

Game Center

If you sign in to Game Center, your level record may be submitted to Apple's Game Center leaderboards. This is handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. You can disable Game Center in iOS Settings.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app may send a local reminder about your Daily Challenge streak. These notifications are scheduled entirely on your device — no data ever leaves it for this purpose. You can disable notifications at any time in iOS Settings.

Purchases

In-app purchases (coin packs) are processed by Apple via the App Store. We never see or store your payment details.

Advertising

Bramsort shows no third-party advertising.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your choices

  • Disable Game Center or notifications via iOS Settings.
  • Reset local data by deleting the app.
